# Order F-3.6a — Truth and flow: dead grants visible, failures preserved, scheduler unblocked

Branch: `followupper-f36a-truth-and-flow`. Workspace: Email Followupper (Doctrine Follow-up). Built from the F-D4 verdict of August 9, 2026; change nothing F-D4 rated healthy.

## Context, fixed

- Production reality: six of twelve accounts hold dead Gmail grants (unauthorized_client) while `/api/gmail/accounts` reports them connected; the sync already detects it (isAuthError → authFailure: true) but nothing flips or surfaces the state. Their auto-queues kept generating: 196 follow-ups, zero sends, 75% of the week's spend, in a revive loop.
- The revive loop: `failed` is not in ACTIVE_FOLLOWUP_STATUSES, so autoQueueAllCampaigns revives every failed row within 15 minutes — status queued, error erased, bodies nulled, rescheduled +1 hour. Every failure erases its own evidence; the failed surface can never show a real rate.
- Head-of-line: the due query passes admin-paused rows (filtered only inside the loop), so user 8's 15 rows permanently occupy 15 of 20 slots.
- Two rows stuck `generating` since 2026-07-21/28, far past RH-1's 6-hour threshold; RH-1 detects but never mutates, and its count goes to an unreadable heartbeat.
- `cron_heartbeats` has been written since Phase 7n and has never been readable by any surface.
- This app sends real email for the live team: every change ships dark-safe, respects windows and caps untouched, and nothing in this order may itself send, generate, or delete evidence.

## Scope

1. **Auth truth.** When a sync tick records authFailure for a user: mark the account's connection state dead (a distinct `auth_dead` state, not a silent disconnect), surface it on `/api/gmail/accounts` and the dashboard Accounts page in plain words ("Gmail connection dead since <date> — reconnect"), and pause that user's auto-queue generation while dead so no money burns against a dead grant. A successful reconnect clears the state automatically. Backfill: on first deploy, one pass marks the currently-dead six from a live probe.
2. **Failures keep their evidence.** A failed row keeps status, error and generated content; revival becomes policy, not amnesia: up to 2 automatic retries with the error history preserved on the row, then the row stays failed and visible, counted on the admin surface. Rows failed due to `auth_dead` are not retried at all until the grant heals. The two July DB-error rows of user 8 are left untouched.
3. **Scheduler flow.** The due query itself excludes admin-paused and auth-dead users, so held rows stop occupying slots; ordering and the 20-slot batch otherwise unchanged. Prove with a fixture reproducing the 15-of-20 starvation and showing it gone.
4. **Stranded generations get teeth.** Rows in `generating` past the RH-1 threshold are moved to failed with a stranded reason (evidence preserved per item 2), freeing their campaigns; the two production rows from July are recovered by this path on deploy, ledgered.
5. **Heartbeats readable.** An admin-authed read endpoint over `cron_heartbeats` (latest N with outcomes and details), and the F-D4 gap closes: liveness becomes checkable without repo access.
6. **Tests**, per this repo's conventions: auth-dead lifecycle including reconnect clearing; retry policy including the no-retry-on-auth-dead rule; the due-query exclusion with the starvation fixture; stranded recovery; heartbeat endpoint auth and shape; and at least one test proven able to fail by reintroducing its defect. No real Gmail, no vendor calls, no sends in any test or smoke.
7. **Report**: the state machine for connection health as built, the retry policy table, the deploy-time backfill's expected effect on the six accounts and two stranded rows, and what the admin now sees that was invisible before.

## Out of scope

The OAuth client itself or any Google Cloud change (the users reconnect by hand); window or cap values; user 10's personal window (Michael's call); the legacy zero-users sync branch (dormant; record as an open item to delete rather than harden); anything in any other repo.

## Ritual and hard rules

Standard fleet set, compact: blast radius first (schema deltas named with rollback), gates, audit rounds (security framing: the new states cannot be spoofed via HTTP, evidence fields render as data, the heartbeat endpoint leaks no tokens), smoke both modes on an isolated database with Gmail stubbed, ledger, merge and push. No publishing, no secret changes, no sends, no vendor calls, no queue mutations outside the named deploy-time recoveries, halt after three failures on one cause, ask rather than guess.
